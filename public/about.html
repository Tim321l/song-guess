About Song Guess Online

What is Song Guess Online?

Song Guess Online is a frantic, real-time multiplayer music trivia game. Challenge your friends to see who can identify the latest hits the fastest! Featuring music from major global artists, C-Pop, J-Pop, English hits, and community-created custom playlists.

How to Play

  • Create a room and invite your friends using the Room Code.
  • Listen to the 30-second audio snippet.
  • Guess the correct song title and artist from the multiple-choice options.
  • The faster you guess correctly, the more points you earn!
  • The player with the most points at the end of the matches wins the crown!

Game Modes

  • Standard Mode: Play a fixed number of rounds. Highest score wins.
  • Elimination (Survival): Every round is a sudden death! Answer wrong and you are knocked out. Last player standing wins.
  • Lyrics Mode: Read the lyrics without the music. Guess the next line correctly!
  • Community Playlists: Connect your Spotify account to import your own custom playlists to challenge the community!

Support the Developer

Song Guess Online is a passionate indie project maintained freely. If you enjoy playing, consider disabling your ad-blocker or sharing the game with your friends!

關於 Song Guess Online

什麼是 Song Guess Online?

Song Guess Online 是一款緊張刺激的實時多人音樂猜謎遊戲。挑戰您的朋友，看看誰能最快辨別出最新的流行歌曲！遊戲收錄了全球主流藝術家、C-Pop、J-Pop、英文熱門歌曲，以及由社區創建的自定義歌單。

如何遊玩

  • 創建房間並使用房間代碼邀請您的朋友。
  • 聆聽 30 秒的音樂片段。
  • 從多項選擇中猜出正確的歌曲名稱和歌手。
  • 猜對的速度越快，獲得的分數就越多！
  • 比賽結束時得分最高的玩家將贏得桂冠！

遊戲模式

  • 標準模式: 遊玩固定輪數。分數最高者獲勝。
  • 淘汰賽 (生存模式): 每一輪都是生死關頭！答錯即被淘汰。最後存活的玩家獲獲勝。
  • 歌詞模式: 在沒有音樂的情況下閱讀歌詞。正確猜出下一回歌詞！
  • 社區歌單: 連接您的 Spotify 帳戶，導入您自己的自定義歌單來挑戰社區！

支持開發者

Song Guess Online 是一個充滿熱情的獨立項目，免費維護。如果您喜歡遊玩，請考慮關閉廣告攔截器或與您的朋友分享遊戲！
