Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you play Song Guess Online, we collect basic game statistics such as your username, total score, and saved favorites. If you connect using a third-party service like Spotify or Google, we only request the necessary permissions to authenticate your session and fetch authorized playlists on your behalf. We do not store your Spotify passwords or broad account access tokens permanently on our servers.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your username and score are used exclusively to power the global leaderboard and your personal statistics. We do not sell, rent, or trade user information to third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the server administrator.

私隱政策

最後更新日期：2026年10月

1. 我們收集的信息

當您遊玩 Song Guess Online 時，我們會收集基本的遊戲統計數據，例如您的用戶名、總分和保存的收藏歌曲。如果您使用 Spotify 或 Google 等第三方服務連接，我們僅請求必要的權限來驗證您的會話並代表您獲取授權的歌單。我們不會在我們的服務器上永久存儲您的 Spotify 密碼或廣泛的帳戶訪問令牌。

2. Cookie 與廣告

我們使用 Google AdSense 等第三方廣告公司在您訪問我們的網站時投放廣告。這些公司可能會使用 Cookie，根據您之前訪問本網站或其他網站的情況來投放廣告。Google 對廣告 Cookie 的使用使其及其合作夥伴能夠根據用戶對您門戶網站和/或互聯網上其他網站的訪問情況向您的用戶投放廣告。

用戶可以通過訪問 Google 的 廣告設置 來選擇退出個性化廣告。

3. 我們如何使用您的信息

您的用戶名和分數僅用於驅動全球排行榜和您的個人統計數據。我們不會向第三方出售、出租或交易用戶信息。

4. 聯繫我們

如果您對我們的私隱政策有任何疑問或建議，請隨時聯繫服務器管理員。
